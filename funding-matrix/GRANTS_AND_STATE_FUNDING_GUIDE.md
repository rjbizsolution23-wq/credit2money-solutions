## 1. Overview of State & Federal Grants for Small Businesses
Grants are **non-dilutive capital**—money that does not require repayment or giving up business equity. Antoine can offer grant research and application assistance as a high-value add-on service or as part of his business funding advisory package.

## 2. Core Government Grant Repositories

### A. Federal Level
1. **Grants.gov**:
   - The central federal database for all discretionary grants funded by 26 federal agencies.
   - Ideal for businesses with workforce development, tech innovation, clean energy, or community initiatives.
2. **Small Business Administration (SBA) Program Grants**:
   - **SBIR / STTR (Small Business Innovation Research)**: High-dollar grants ($150,000 to $1,000,000+) for technological, scientific, or digital platforms.
   - **State Trade Expansion Program (STEP)**: Helps small businesses export goods and enter international markets.

### B. State & Regional Level (Wisconsin & Midwest Focus)
*Since Antoine is based in the 414 area code (Milwaukee / Wisconsin):*
1. **Wisconsin Economic Development Corporation (WEDC)**:
   - **Main Street Bounceback Grants**: Grants up to $10,000 for small businesses occupying commercial spaces in downtown or commercial corridors.
   - **Diverse Business Development Grants**: Support for minority-owned, women-owned, and veteran-owned enterprises.
2. **Wisconsin Small Business Development Center (SBDC)**:
   - Free consulting, grant matching, and loan package preparation across 13 university centers in Wisconsin.
3. **Milwaukee Economic Development Corporation (MEDC)**:
   - Low-interest second mortgage and gap financing loans paired with local bank participation.

### C. Corporate & Foundation Non-Profit Grants
- **Amber Grant for Women**: $10,000 monthly grant + $25,000 annual grant.
- **FedEx Small Business Grant**: Up to $30,000 + FedEx services credit.
- **NASE (National Association for the Self-Employed)**: $4,000 growth grants awarded quarterly.
- **Hello Alice Small Business Grants**: Aggregates enterprise partner grants ($5,000 - $50,000) from DoorDash, Antares, and Mastercard.

## 3. Grant Application Formula
To win grants for clients:
1. **Clear Problem Statement**: How does the business solve a local community problem, create jobs, or expand workforce skills?
2. **Itemized Budget**: Exactly how will every dollar be spent (e.g. $4,000 inventory, $3,000 software/equipment, $3,000 marketing)?
3. **DUNS & SAM.gov Registration**: Mandatory for federal and state grants. Ensure client has their Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) registered.
