## The 7 Golden Rules of 0% APR Funding Approval
Before submitting any client into a credit card stacking round, their personal and business profile must pass this algorithmic pre-flight checklist:

### 1. Personal Credit Profile Requirements
- [ ] **FICO Score**: Minimum **680+** on all three bureaus (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion). **720+** is the sweet spot for maximum initial limits ($25k+ per card).
- [ ] **Revolving Credit Utilization**: Total personal utilization must be **below 9%**. No single credit card should exceed 28% utilization.
- [ ] **Recent Hard Inquiries**:
  - Maximum **2 inquiries** in the last 6 months on the bureau being pulled.
  - Maximum **4 inquiries** in the last 12 months.
- [ ] **Derogatory Items**: Zero (0) open collections, active charge-offs, or late payments within the past 24 months.
- [ ] **Primary Trade Line Depth**:
  - At least **2 primary credit cards** with minimum limits of **$5,000+** (the higher the personal limit, the higher the business limit will match).
  - Minimum credit history age: **2+ years** of continuous reporting.

---

### 2. Business Entity Foundation
- [ ] **Active & In Good Standing**: LLC or Corporation registered with the Secretary of State.
- [ ] **Federal EIN**: EIN Confirmation Letter (Form CP 575 or 147C) from the IRS.
- [ ] **Commercial Address**: Physical commercial office or verified virtual office address (No PO Boxes or residential homes if targeting tier-1 banks).
- [ ] **Business Phone & 411 Directory**: Dedicated business phone number (VOIP or toll-free) listed on ListYourself.net and 411 directories.
- [ ] **Domain & Professional Email**: Active website (`.com` or `.org`) and domain email (e.g. `antoine@credit2money.org` — not a free `@gmail.com`).
- [ ] **Dedicated Business Checking Account**: Opened in the business name with at least $500–$1,000 opening deposit.
- [ ] **NAICS Code Compliance**: Ensure NAICS industry classification code is non-restricted (Avoid high-risk codes like real estate investing, gambling, firearms, or speculative trading; choose Management Consulting, Marketing, or Business Services).
