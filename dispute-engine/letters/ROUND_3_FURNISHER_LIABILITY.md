# 📄 ROUND 3: DIRECT FURNISHER LIABILITY DEMAND (623 NOTICE)
**Credit 2 Money Solutions** | Dispute Document Library  
*Sent directly to the original creditor or collection agency under FCRA § 1681s-2b.*

---

## Legal Foundation
Under **15 U.S.C. § 1681s-2(b)**, once a data furnisher receives notice of a dispute from a consumer reporting agency (or from the consumer following a bureau dispute), the furnisher has an independent statutory duty to:
1. Conduct an investigation with respect to the disputed information;
2. Review all relevant information provided by the agency;
3. Report the results of the investigation to the consumer reporting agency; and
4. If an item is found to be inaccurate or incomplete, promptly modify, delete, or permanently block the reporting of that item.

A furnisher that merely verifies inaccurate records without human documentation is liable for statutory and actual civil damages under **15 U.S.C. § 1681n**.

If this account was written off for corporate tax purposes (1099-C) or sold to a third-party debt buyer, reporting a balance is a direct violation of federal reporting regulations and constitutes false credit balance inflation.
2. Inconsistent Date of First Delinquency (DOFD): The DOFD you supplied to Experian contradicts the date reported to Equifax and TransUnion.
3. Lack of Physical Verification: You have not produced the signed credit application, cardholder agreement, or a zero-balance closing statement bearing my authorization.

Conduct an immediate forensic investigation into your internal records. If you are unable to produce the original signed documentation and an unbroken audit ledger substantiating every dollar reported within thirty (30) days:

You are legally required under 15 U.S.C. § 1681s-2(b)(1)(E) to immediately notify all three nationwide credit reporting agencies to **COMPLETELY DELETE THIS DEROGATORY TRADE LINE**.

Govern yourselves accordingly.
