## When to Use This Letter
Credit bureaus frequently respond to Round 1 factual disputes with generic stall tactics:
1. *"We received a suspicious request / Please verify your identity."* (Even though ID and utility bills were enclosed).
2. *"We deemed your dispute frivolous or irrelevant under FCRA § 1681i(a)(3)."*
3. *"We suspect a third-party credit repair company prepared this letter."*

These stall letters are an automated tactic to bypass their statutory 30-day investigation deadline. **Under federal law, they are strictly prohibited from doing this when clear factual errors were stated.**
