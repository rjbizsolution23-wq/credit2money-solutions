# 📄 ROUND 2B: METHOD OF VERIFICATION (MOV) DEMAND
**Credit 2 Money Solutions** | Dispute Document Library  
*Specifically engineered when a bureau claims an item was "Verified" or "Validated".*

---

## When to Use This Letter
When the bureau replies to a dispute stating: *"This item has been verified as accurate"*, but they do not provide any physical proof.

Under **FCRA 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(6)(B)(iii)** and **§ 1681i(a)(7)**, the bureau is **legally mandated** to provide the consumer with the **exact method of verification**, including the business name, address, phone number, and specific individual contacted, within fifteen (15) days of the request.

---

```text
[Date]

[Client Full Name]

DOB: [MM/DD/YYYY] | SSN: [XXX-XX-XXXX]

To:
[Bureau Name - Equifax / Experian / TransUnion]
Compliance & Reinvestigation Department
[Bureau Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

SENT VIA CERTIFIED MAIL RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED: [Tracking Number]

SUBJECT: FORMAL REQUEST FOR METHOD OF VERIFICATION PURSUANT TO 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(6)(B)(iii) & § 1681i(a)(7)
REFERENCE DISPUTE FILE #: [Dispute Reference Number]

Dear Dispute Director,

I am in receipt of your reinvestigation results dated [Date of Results Letter] regarding my dispute of the following trade line:

Creditor / Furnisher Name: [Creditor Name]
Account Number: [Account Number]
Result Stated by You: "Verified as Accurate"

I hereby request that you provide me with the exact **Method of Verification (MOV)** used to verify this inaccurate information, as strictly guaranteed by federal law under **15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(6)(B)(iii)** and **15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(7)**.

PURSUANT TO SECTION 1681i(a)(7), YOU ARE REQUIRED TO PROVIDE:
1. The exact name, address, and telephone number of each person contacted at [Creditor Name] with whom you verified the alleged information.
2. The specific documents reviewed (e.g., original signed consumer credit agreement, payment receipts, accounting ledger). Automated e-OSCAR computerized 2-digit verification codes (such as code '01') do NOT constitute a reasonable reinvestigation as defined in Cushman v. Trans Union Corp. and Stevenson v. TRW Inc.
3. The specific date and time the verification took place.

STATUTORY TIMELINE:
Under 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(7), you must provide this Method of Verification description to me **within not later than 15 days** after the receipt of this request.

If you cannot produce the specific human point of contact and documentary proof within fifteen (15) days, you have failed your statutory reinvestigation duty and this derogatory account must be **DELETED IMMEDIATELY**.

Sincerely,

___________________________________
[Client Full Name]

Enclosures: Photo ID, Utility Bill, Copy of Reinvestigation Results
```
