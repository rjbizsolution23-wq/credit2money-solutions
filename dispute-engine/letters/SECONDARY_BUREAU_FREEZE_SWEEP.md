## Why Secondary Bureaus Matter
When a client disputes a collection, bankruptcy, judgment, or charge-off with Experian, Equifax, or TransUnion, the Big 3 do not keep microfilms. **They verify through secondary background data brokers** (primarily LexisNexis, CoreLogic, Innovis, and SageStream).

If you **freeze** and **clean up** the secondary bureaus FIRST, the Big 3 have nowhere to cross-reference the data. When the 30-day investigation clock runs out, they are legally forced to **DELETE**.
