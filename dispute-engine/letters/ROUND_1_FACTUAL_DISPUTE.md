## Template 1A: Hard Inquiries Removal (FCRA § 1681b)
*(Use for unauthorized or unattached hard inquiries)*

```text
[Date]

[Client Full Name]
[Client Address]
[City, State, ZIP]
DOB: [MM/DD/YYYY] | SSN: [XXX-XX-XXXX]

To:
[Bureau Name - Equifax / Experian / TransUnion]
[Bureau Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

SENT VIA CERTIFIED MAIL RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED: [Tracking Number]

SUBJECT: FORMAL NOTICE OF UNAUTHORIZED INQUIRIES & DEMAND FOR IMMEDIATE DELETION (FCRA § 1681b)

Dear Dispute Department,

Upon a forensic audit of my consumer credit file, I identified the following unauthorized hard inquiries that were placed on my file without my express written consent or permissible purpose as strictly mandated by the Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1681b:

1. Creditor Name: [Creditor Name] | Date of Inquiry: [Date]
2. Creditor Name: [Creditor Name] | Date of Inquiry: [Date]
3. Creditor Name: [Creditor Name] | Date of Inquiry: [Date]

Under 15 U.S.C. § 1681b, a consumer reporting agency may only furnish a credit report when it has reason to believe that the recipient has a permissible statutory purpose and explicit consumer authorization. I did not apply for credit with these entities, nor did I provide written consent authorizing them to pull my consumer file. 

Furthermore, under FCRA § 1681e(b), you are legally obligated to follow reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy. These unverified inquiries are causing tangible damage to my credit score and financing capability.

DEMAND:
I demand that you immediately investigate and permanently remove these unauthorized hard inquiries from my credit file within thirty (30) days as required under 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(1). Provide me an updated copy of my consumer disclosure upon completion.

Sincerely,

___________________________________
[Client Full Name]

Enclosures: Copy of Driver's License, Utility Bill (Proof of Identity)
```

## Template 1B: Third-Party Collections Attack (FDCPA & Metro 2)

```text
[Date]

[Client Full Name]
[Client Address]
[City, State, ZIP]
DOB: [MM/DD/YYYY] | SSN: [XXX-XX-XXXX]

To:
[Bureau Name - Equifax / Experian / TransUnion]
[Bureau Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

SENT VIA CERTIFIED MAIL RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED: [Tracking Number]

SUBJECT: NOTICE OF INACCURATE REPORTING & DISPUTE OF UNVERIFIED COLLECTION ACCOUNT (15 U.S.C. § 1681i)

Dear Dispute Department,

I am writing to formally dispute the following inaccurate collection account appearing on my credit disclosure. I have never entered into any contractual agreement with this debt collector, nor have they proven chain of title or legal standing to collect:

Collection Agency: [Collection Agency Name]
Account Number: [Account Number Masked]
Reported Balance: [Balance]

CRITICAL COMPLIANCE DEFECTS:
1. Absence of Contractual Privity: I have never signed an agreement with [Collection Agency Name]. Under FDCPA 15 U.S.C. § 1692g, a debt collector must provide complete verification of the debt, including the original creditor's contract with my signature.
2. Metro 2 Classification Error: This account is being misreported as an active open account, when in fact collection accounts must adhere strictly to Account Type 48.
3. Balance Discrepancy: The balance reported does not reflect an itemized accounting of principal, interest, and unauthorized collection fees.

Under 15 U.S.C. § 1681i, you are required to conduct a reasonable reinvestigation of this disputed item. If the data furnisher cannot produce complete physical evidence and chain of assignment within thirty (30) days, this derogatory trade line must be completely deleted from my consumer file.

Sincerely,

___________________________________
[Client Full Name]
```
