## 1. The Core Law of Factual Disputing
Credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) and data furnishers (banks, collection agencies) are legally mandated by the **Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681)** to maintain **maximum possible accuracy** (FCRA § 1681e(b)).

Bureaus do not store documents; they store digital data strings formatted in the **Metro 2® Electronic Reporting Format** developed by the Consumer Data Industry Association (CDIA). When data elements contradict each other across bureaus, violate Metro 2 rules, or lack complete physical verification, the item is legally non-compliant and **MUST BE DELETED**.

## 2. The 5 Metro 2® Compliance Vulnerabilities to Exploit
When auditing any client credit report, search for these fatal reporting errors:

1. **Date of First Delinquency (DOFD) Inconsistency (Metro 2 Field 25)**:
   - The DOFD governs the statutory 7-year obsolescence clock (FCRA § 1681c). If Equifax reports DOFD as `03/2021` and Experian reports `06/2021`, the reporting is factually contradictory and legally unverified.
2. **Account Status `97` vs. Current Balance Conflict**:
   - Status Code `97` indicates an unpaid charge-off. If the original creditor sold the debt to a collection agency, the original creditor's balance **MUST BE REPORTED AS ZERO ($0)**. If they report a balance AND a collection agency also reports a balance, that is **double balance reporting** (illegal balance inflation).
3. **Scheduled Monthly Payment on Closed / Charged-Off Accounts**:
   - A closed or charged-off account has no active contract requiring ongoing monthly payments. Reporting a "$150/mo scheduled payment" falsely deflates the consumer's debt-to-income (DTI) ratio.
4. **Payment History Profile (PHP) Gaps & Shifts**:
   - 24-month payment history grid strings must match exact calendar months. Any gaps, phantom late marks, or misaligned months violate Metro 2 field formatting guidelines.
5. **Collection Agency Account Type Errors**:
   - Collection accounts must be reported as **Account Type `48` (Collection Agency/Attorney)**. Reporting as an "Open Account", "Factoring Company", or "Revolving" account is a direct violation of Metro 2 and FDCPA § 807(2) (false representation of the character or legal status of the debt).

## 3. The 4-Stage Escalation Protocol
- **Stage 1 (Day 1–30)**: Direct Bureau Factual Dispute via Certified Mail with Return Receipt.
- **Stage 2 (Day 31–60)**: Stall-Breaker Notice (if bureau sends "frivolous" letter) or Method of Verification (MOV) demand under FCRA § 1681i(a)(6)(B)(iii).
- **Stage 3 (Day 61–90)**: Direct Furnisher Liability Demand under FCRA § 1681s-2b (623 notice).
- **Stage 4 (Day 91+)**: Administrative CFPB/FTC Complaints and FCRA Litigation Packet handoff to consumer attorney.
