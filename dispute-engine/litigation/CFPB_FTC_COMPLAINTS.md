## Why CFPB Complaints Work
The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) is the primary federal regulator over Equifax, Experian, TransUnion, and major banks. When you submit a complaint via `consumerfinance.gov`:
1. It bypasses lower-level customer service and routes directly to the bureau's **Executive Legal Compliance Escalation Team**.
2. The bureau/bank is federally mandated to submit a formal written response within **15 calendar days**.
3. Patterns of non-compliance subject the institution to federal consent orders and civil monetary penalties.

## 1. CFPB Online Complaint Submission Blueprint

### Complaint Parameters:
- **Product:** Credit reporting, credit repair services, or other personal consumer reports
- **Sub-product:** Credit reporting
- **Problem Category:** Problem with a company's investigation of an existing problem
- **Sub-issue:** Their investigation did not fix an error on your report

### Narrative Draft (Copy & Paste for Client):
```text
On [Date of Round 1 Dispute], I submitted a formal written dispute via Certified Mail (Tracking #: [Tracking Number]) to [Bureau Name - Equifax/Experian/TransUnion] identifying specific, documented inaccuracies regarding [Creditor/Collector Name, Account #].

Specifically, the account is reporting conflicting Metro 2 data, including [e.g., an inaccurate Date of First Delinquency, balance inflation on a charged-off account, or an unverified collection without contractual assignment].

Under 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(1), the bureau had 30 days to conduct a reasonable reinvestigation. Instead of performing a substantive investigation, the bureau either [issued a pretextual frivolous stall letter / blindly verified the unverified account via an automated e-OSCAR computer code without reviewing documentation].

Subsequent requests for the specific Method of Verification pursuant to 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(7) have been ignored or met with automated template letters.

The bureau has failed to follow reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy as mandated by FCRA 15 U.S.C. § 1681e(b). This continuous reporting of unverified derogatory information is actively damaging my credit reputation and preventing me from obtaining business credit.

DESIRED RESOLUTION:
Immediate and permanent deletion of the unverified trade line ([Creditor Name], Account #: [Account #]) from my credit report, and an updated copy of my consumer disclosure reflecting the deletion.
```

### Mandatory Attachments to Upload:
1. PDF of original dispute letter.
2. USPS Certified Mail delivery confirmation with timestamp and signature.
3. Bureau response letter showing failure to correct.
4. Client Photo ID and utility bill.

## 2. FTC Report Submission (ReportFraud.ftc.gov)
*Use for identity theft, unauthorized hard inquiries, or deceptive collection practices under FDCPA.*
- File an FTC Fraud / Identity Theft Report at `reportfraud.ftc.gov`.
- Once generated, an official **FTC Identity Theft Report** carries statutory force under **FCRA 15 U.S.C. § 1681c-2**:
  - The credit bureaus are **legally mandated to block** the reporting of any disputed item within **4 business days** of receiving an official FTC Identity Theft Report and proof of identity!
