## 1. The Legal Foundation for Suing Bureaus & Furnishers
When bureaus or collection agencies refuse to delete provably inaccurate items after proper rounds of dispute, they enter the realm of **Willful Non-Compliance** under federal law.

### Key Statutory Provisions:
- **15 U.S.C. § 1681n (Civil Liability for Willful Noncompliance)**:
  - Any person who willfully fails to comply with any requirement is liable to that consumer for:
    1. **Statutory damages of not less than $100 and not more than $1,000 per violation**;
    2. Punitive damages as the court allows; and
    3. **Mandatory reasonable attorney's fees and court costs**.
- **15 U.S.C. § 1681o (Civil Liability for Negligent Noncompliance)**:
  - Actual damages sustained by the consumer + attorney fees.
- **Attorney Fee-Shifting Rule**:
  - Because the FCRA and FDCPA mandate that the defendant pays the consumer's legal fees upon winning or settling, **consumer rights litigation attorneys take these cases on 100% contingency (ZERO cost out-of-pocket to the client or Credit 2 Money Solutions)!**

## 2. The 5-Step Litigation Audit Checklist
Before referring a client file to a consumer litigation attorney, verify that the following **unbroken paper trail** is in hand:

- [ ] **Proof of Inaccuracy**: Credit report copies showing the clear factual contradiction (e.g. conflicting balance, misreported DOFD, closed account with monthly payment).
- [ ] **Proof of Bureau Notice**: The initial dispute letter sent via USPS Certified Mail with USPS tracking proof showing the bureau received it.
- [ ] **Proof of Furnisher Notice**: The bureau's e-OSCAR record or direct 623 notice to the furnisher.
- [ ] **Proof of Failure to Reinvestigate**: The bureau's response letter verifying the inaccurate account or failing to provide the Method of Verification within 15 days.
- [ ] **Proof of Concrete Harm (Spokeo / TransUnion v. Ramirez Standard)**:
  - Proof that the client was denied a credit card, auto loan, or business funding, or was forced to pay higher interest rates directly due to the inaccurate trade line.

## 3. Attorney Referral Handoff Packet Structure
Assemble the following documents into a single PDF dossier named: `FCRA_Litigation_Packet_[ClientName]_[BureauName].pdf`:

1. **Cover Summary Sheet**:
   - Client Name, Contact Info, State of Residence.
   - Defendant Name(s) (e.g. Experian Information Solutions, Inc. + Portfolio Recovery Associates, LLC).
   - Summary of Violations (List of FCRA §§ violated).
   - Calculation of statutory damages requested.
2. **Exhibit A**: Initial Credit Report showing erroneous trade line.
3. **Exhibit B**: Round 1 Dispute Letter + USPS Certified Mail Tracking Slip.
4. **Exhibit C**: Bureau's response letter (verification or frivolous stall).
5. **Exhibit D**: Round 2 MOV Demand / Direct Furnisher 623 Dispute.
6. **Exhibit E**: Adverse Action Notice / Denial letter from lender demonstrating concrete harm.
