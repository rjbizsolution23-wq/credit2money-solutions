## 1. System Overview
Antoine noted in his first voice message:  
*"I got my social media set up but haven't made any posts yet, so I want to kind of fill my posts up automatically using AI bots on my web page as well, AI assistant with my credit repair company."*

This guide sets up an automated publishing engine connecting the **30-Day Content Calendar** to all of Antoine's social accounts (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook Page, X/Twitter, TikTok) with zero daily manual effort.

---

## 2. Recommended Auto-Posting Stacks

### Option A: GoHighLevel (GHL) Social Planner (Recommended)
If Antoine uses GoHighLevel (or an RJ white-label GHL subaccount):
1. Navigate to **Marketing > Social Planner**.
2. Connect accounts: Facebook Page, Instagram Business, LinkedIn Profile, X/Twitter, and TikTok.
3. Click **CSV Upload / Bulk Schedule**.
4. Import the formatted `30_DAY_CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` table.
5. Set the schedule for **1 post daily at 10:00 AM CST** and **1 reel daily at 6:00 PM CST**.
6. The entire month is queued in under 10 minutes.

### Option B: Buffer / Publer via Make.com
If running standalone:
1. Connect Antoine's social channels to **Buffer.com** (Free/low-cost tier).
2. Create a free scenario in **Make.com**:
   - Trigger: Google Sheet / AirTable row updated (containing calendar posts).
   - Action: Buffer "Create Status Update".
3. Auto-publishes across all channels simultaneously.
