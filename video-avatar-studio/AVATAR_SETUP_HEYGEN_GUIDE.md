## 2. Step-by-Step Recording Blueprint for Antoine
To create an elite, indistinguishable AI twin of Antoine:

### Step 1: Equipment & Framing
- **Camera**: iPhone 14 Pro or newer in 4K at 30fps (or mirrorless camera).
- **Lighting**: Clean 3-point lighting or a ring light facing forward with soft diffused light (avoid harsh shadows on the face).
- **Audio**: Crisp lapel / wireless microphone (e.g. DJI Mic or Rode Wireless GO) recorded in a quiet room with zero echo.
- **Background**: Modern office setting, neutral slate background, or clean interior matching Credit 2 Money Solutions branding.
- **Wardrobe**: Fitted suit jacket, collared dress shirt, or sharp branded executive apparel.

### Step 2: The 2-Minute Training Video
- Look directly into the camera lens with natural eye contact.
- Speak naturally for 2 minutes reading a standard corporate script.
- Pause between sentences for 1 second with a closed mouth.
- Use subtle, natural hand gestures below chest level.

### Step 3: HeyGen Setup
1. Sign up at `heygen.com` (Pro or Enterprise tier).
2. Navigate to **Avatars > Create Avatar > Instant Avatar**.
3. Upload the 2-minute 4K training video.
4. Record the 10-second identity consent statement on webcam.
5. In **Voices**, upload a clean 60-second audio clip of Antoine talking to create his **Instant Voice Clone**.
6. Turnaround time: Under 15 minutes!
